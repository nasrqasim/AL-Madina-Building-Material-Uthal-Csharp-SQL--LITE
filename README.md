## ✨ Key Features

- **Sales Management & POS Counter**:
  - Fast Retail Checkout & Invoice Generation
  - A4 Thermal & Standard Laser Receipt Printing with Custom Banners
  - Sale Return & Customer Balance Tracking
- **Purchases & Vendor Management**:
  - Vendor Purchase Invoices & Purchase Returns
  - Integrated Freight / Vehicle Transport Charges added to Net Total
  - Vendor Ledger & Outstanding Balance Management
- **Inventory & Warehouse Tracking**:
  - Chart of Inventory & Stock Management
  - Item Categories, Units, and Reorder Point Tracking
- **Financials & Accounting**:
  - Customer Ledgers, Vendor Ledgers, and Cash/Bank Accounts
  - Expense Management & Employee Salaries
  - Real-time Balance Due & Advance Balance Updates
- **Security & Database**:
  - Offline-first SQLite database (`Company.db`) with WAL Mode
  - Automatic Database Backup & Restore System

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework**: .NET 8.0 WPF (Windows Presentation Foundation)
- **Architecture**: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) using `CommunityToolkit.Mvvm`
- **Database**: SQLite with EF Core (`Microsoft.EntityFrameworkCore.Sqlite`)
- **Printing**: Native WPF FlowDocument A4 & Thermal Printing Service
